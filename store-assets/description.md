## Description (long)

Paste into the "Description" field.

```text
QuickNotes turns any web page into paper you can write on. Select text to highlight it, pin a sticky note next to the passage that matters, and find everything again the next time you visit — even if the page changed a little in between.

HIGHLIGHT
• Select text and a small toolbar appears: yellow, green, blue or pink.
• Or right-click a selection and choose "Highlight with QuickNotes".
• Click a highlight to change its color, add a note to it, or delete it.

STICKY NOTES
• Pin notes anywhere on a page with "Add note", the popup, or Alt+N.
• Bold, italic, bulleted and numbered lists (Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I).
• Drag, resize, minimize and recolor them. They stay where you put them.

FIND IT AGAIN
• Highlights are anchored to the text itself, with its surrounding context, not to pixel positions. If a passage can no longer be found, QuickNotes lists it as "orphaned" instead of highlighting the wrong words.
• The side panel lists the highlights and notes of the page you are on; click one to scroll to it.
• "All notes" collects every page: full-text search, filters by color and by site.

EXPORT AND BACKUP
• Export one page or all pages to Markdown, ready for your notes app.
• Export to JSON and import it again on another computer (imports merge; nothing is deleted).

YOUR PAGE, YOUR RULES
• "Pause on this site" turns QuickNotes off on any domain.
• Options: default color, show or hide the selection toolbar, paused sites, light/dark theme, and a typed confirmation before deleting everything.
• Everything QuickNotes draws lives in its own isolated layer, so websites cannot break it and it cannot break websites.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no servers, no analytics, no network requests at all.
• Your notes are stored by Chrome on your device (settings follow your Chrome profile if you use sync).
• Minimal permissions: QuickNotes only runs on a tab when you use it there. Restoring notes automatically on every site is optional and asks for permission first.

Available in English and Spanish.
```
